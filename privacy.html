Tuna Tuna

Tuner

Privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Tuna Tuna is a guitar tuner that runs on your device, both on the website and in the Android app. This policy describes what the app touches and what it leaves alone.

Microphone

Tuna Tuna uses your microphone only in real time on your device to detect pitch. Audio is processed strictly on-device and is never recorded, stored, transmitted, or shared. The sound is analyzed in the moment to estimate a note, then discarded. There is no recording, no upload, and no server that receives audio.

Before the first microphone prompt, the app shows this explanation and waits for you to continue. That acknowledgement is saved only in local storage on the device.

Saved on this device

Tuner preferences are stored only locally on the device:

  • A4 calibration
  • Tuning preset
  • Tuner mode (guitar or chromatic)
  • Whether you have already seen the microphone explanation

Those values stay in the browser or app storage on this device. They are not sent anywhere. Clearing site data, or uninstalling the app, removes them. The offline copy of the app stores Tuna Tuna’s own files on the device so the tuner can open without a network. That cache is not a profile of you.

What Tuna Tuna does not do

Tuna Tuna has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not collect personal data, and it does not share personal data. It does not require an account. The Android package does not upload tuner audio or preferences.

Optional tip

Tuna Tuna is free, with every feature unlocked. An optional tip goes directly to the creator and unlocks nothing. If a tip link is available and you choose to open it, it opens in your browser. The payment is handled by that payment provider under their own privacy policy. Tuna Tuna does not see or store payment details.
